02_PREPRODUCTION

Creative planning and the authoritative production brief.

Contents:
brief - The authoritative creative brief; keep revisions here.
script - Scripts and approved wording.
references - Visual and technical inspiration.
moodboards - Visual direction and look exploration.
storyboards - Framing and action across the commercial.
shot_list - Shot IDs, descriptions, timing, and status.
asset_list - Required models, graphics, and other assets.
approvals - Records of approved creative decisions.
